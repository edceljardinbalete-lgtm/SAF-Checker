Help & Tutorial

everything on this page runs locally — nothing you load is ever sent anywhere

Interactive tour

Prefer a guided walkthrough? This highlights each part of the tool in order, like a quick in-app demo — click below anytime.

Loading logs

Drag a .zip, .tgz/.tar.gz, or .tar archive onto the drop zone, or click it to browse. Archives nested inside other archives are unwrapped automatically. Accepted logs: system.dayYYYYMMDD.log exports and scaapp*.log pinpad OS log bundles.

After your first archive loads, use "+ Add another archive" to merge in more stores/exports into the same session, or "Clear & start over" to reset everything.

Terms

SAF — queued, awaiting retry (shown as Pending)
CSAF — host approved on retry (shown as Approved)
ISAF — host declined / retries exhausted (shown as Declined)

Dashboard tab

Click the Pending / Declined / Approved stat tiles to jump straight to a filtered Transactions view. The chart below shows created vs. declined vs. approved counts per day.

Detailed Transactions tab

Live summary tiles at the top always reflect whatever's currently filtered below. Filter by status, source (Systemday vs. OS Log), and terminal serial number (shown once a serial is detected). Search matches invoice, STAN, card, or serial. Click any row to open its full detail drawer, including every retry attempt and the raw log lines behind it.

Copying & exporting

Use Copy to copy the visible table straight to your clipboard (handy for pasting into an email or ticket). Use Export to Excel for a two-sheet workbook — a Summary sheet (totals, per-terminal and per-day breakdowns) plus the full Transactions sheet — scoped to everything on the Dashboard, or to whatever's currently filtered/searched on the Transactions tab.

Troubleshooting

"No system.day / scaapp files found" — the archive didn't contain recognizable log filenames.

"This browser is too old…" — update to a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari; this tool needs native DecompressionStream support.

A transaction looks stuck on Pending — load the archive covering the day it resolved into the same session; it'll merge in automatically.

Copy button doesn't do anything — some browsers block clipboard access when this file is opened directly from disk; try selecting the table text manually instead.
